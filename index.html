About Sukoon-e-Qalb

Sukoon-e-Qalb · سکونِ قلب

Sukoon-e-Qalb is a calm place to listen and learn — so we may understand the teachings of the Hadith and the Quran, and find a little peace of heart. Yahan aap tamam lectures ek hi jagah dekh sakte hain.

Hamari chaar series:

  • Riyaad Us Saliheen — our main weekly series. A new lecture is added every Sunday.
  • Positive Parenting in Islam — raising children with love, mercy, and Islamic values.
  • Surah Al-Kahf — a verse-by-verse explanation of this blessed surah.
  • Youm-e-Arafah — a lecture on the blessed day of Arafah.

Agar yeh lectures aap ke kaam aayein, family aur doston ke saath share kar dijiye. May this effort reach those who need it, and bring a little sukoon to our homes.

Allah hum sab ko ilm-e-nafe ata farmaye. Ameen.
